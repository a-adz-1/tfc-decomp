Why does voice_enable 1 lag me when people talk?

Their voice is added to your update packets, so on a low rate the server holds back (chokes) your updates while they talk. Watch choke in net_graph 3 and try rate 100000.

Full explanation →

Why am I smooth but everyone else looks jittery?

You're drawn from your own prediction; everyone else is timed by a game clock that's constantly nudged by up to 7.5 ms per frame to keep up with the server.

Full explanation →

My frame-time graphs are perfect. Why does it still feel choppy?

Your PC draws frames evenly, but 100 fps on a 144 Hz screen can't line up without G-Sync/FreeSync, and vsync is on by default in the 25th client.

Full explanation →

Why do I get stuck on teammates, then snap back?

The server checks you against where others were on your screen, so players can end up overlapping; hold jump, duck or fire to get freed straight away.

Full explanation →

Why did the sentry shoot him and not me?

Sentries pick the first valid player by player slot, not the nearest, and keep that target. It's been that way since 1999.

Full explanation →

Why do my ramp slides sometimes fail, or stop dead?

Your fps decides whether you touch the floor for a frame before the ramp, which changes your speed; at very high fps the ramp bug can zero it. fps_max 100 gives the classic feel.

Full explanation →

Can the ramp-slide problems be fixed?

The dead stops (the ramp bug) can, with a one-line server fix. Slides changing with fps can't be fixed from the server alone without changing every slide.

Full explanation →

How do we fix getting stuck on walls?

Make the server free stuck players straight away (it currently waits up to a second), fix the ramp bug, and optionally let corners slide instead of stopping you dead.

What settings should I use?

cl_updaterate 102, cl_cmdrate above your fps, rate 100000, ex_interp 0.02 or more, fps_max 100, gl_vsync 0, true fullscreen.

Does the 25th Anniversary client hurt me on OTFC?

No: netcode and movement are the same. Turn vsync off; on widescreen it also shows you more to the sides than the old client.

Was the game smoother back in the day?

Same netcode since 2000; what changed is the hardware: CRTs at matching refresh rates, true fullscreen, no vsync by default.
