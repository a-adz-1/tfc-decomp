Voice and lag

Why turning voice on can make the game lag while other people talk, and what to do about it.

1What happens to someone's voice

  1. Their game records the microphone, compresses it (the OTFC servers use the Speex codec) and adds it to the commands they send the server.
  2. The server copies it straight into the next update packet of every player who can hear them, alongside the normal game update.
  3. Your game reads the packet, decompresses the voice on its main thread (the same loop that draws your frames) and plays it through the game's sound system.

2Cause 1: your updates get choked

The server limits how many bytes a second it sends you to your rate setting. After each packet it works out when it may send the next one: packet size ÷ rate seconds later. Voice makes your packets bigger, so the next allowed send moves later. When it isn't allowed yet, the update due that frame is skipped (net_graph calls that choke) and the next one carries the state a little later.

  • One talker adds a few kilobytes a second; several at once add several times that.
  • Normal updates at ~100 a second already use a good part of a low rate, so voice on top tips you over.
  • Result: other players move jerkily and your shots feel late for as long as people talk, then it recovers.

The OTFC servers allow high rates (they cap at 100000 bytes a second when they limit you), so this is fixed on your side by raising rate.

3Cause 2: decoding on the main thread

Each incoming voice packet is decompressed immediately, inside the game's main loop, before the next frame. On a modern CPU one packet takes very little time, but it adds up with several talkers, and it lands on the same thread that's racing to start each frame on time. Slow or misbehaving sound output can make it worse. voice_profile 1 prints how long it takes on your PC.

4Things that feel like lag but aren't

  • Game sound drops when someone talks. voice_overdrive 2 (default) lowers other sounds while voice plays, so footsteps and shots get quieter for a moment (fading back over 0.4 s). voice_overdrive 1 turns this off.
  • Voice starts a moment late. The game buffers a little voice before it starts playing so it doesn't stutter. That's deliberate.

5What to do

SettingWhy
rate 100000Leaves room for voice on top of ~100 updates a second, so nothing gets choked
cl_updaterate 102Full updates; with a high rate voice no longer costs you any
voice_overdrive 1Keeps game sounds at full volume while people talk
voice_enable 0Last resort: no voice played at all (we haven't checked whether the server then stops sending it to you)

6How to check which it is

  1. Turn on net_graph 3.
  2. Have a few people talk at once. If choke rises while they talk, it's cause 1: raise rate and try again.
  3. If choke stays at 0 but it still hitches, turn on voice_profile 1: high decode times point at cause 2 or your sound setup.

This comes from reading the game's code; we haven't measured it in game yet. Tell us in the OTFC Discord what you see.
